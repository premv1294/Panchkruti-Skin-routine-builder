# Changelog
## 1.2.0 — 2026-09-22
- Added an inline gift-box product selector on Skin Guide and Concerns, plus /gift-guide for direct entry.
- Added per-product instructions, source routine reference, selected-product pairing checks and copyable selection links.
- Unsupported combinations remain unassessed; selection never creates a new product-stacking routine.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-22
- Grouped glycolic and lactic acids under AHA, and salicylic acid under BHA.
- Added family pages, child-aware search and grouped product ingredient links.
- Preserved individual ingredient URLs and exact formulation associations; family product counts are deduplicated.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-19
- Made the incomplete Replit export independently installable with pinned catalog replacements and a pnpm lockfile.
- Retained the existing stack, colour tokens and font families.
- Split the single-page guide into routed modules, reusable components, data and services.
- Added a six-question routine builder, source-defined AM/PM routines, explanations and transparent unavailable states.
- Added local saved routines with validation, version checks, removal and storage-failure handling.
- Imported appropriate workbook product facts; recorded source mappings, current overrides and superseded formulations.
- Excluded discontinued Acne Defense Serum from recommendations and active product listings.
- Added searchable product and ingredient libraries, source-preserving full INCI and partial-formula distinction.
- Replaced unverified compatibility assertions with an explicitly reviewable rule system.
- Added safe commerce integration points and a no-op analytics abstraction with no questionnaire answer payloads.
- Added static route rendering, route metadata, QR routine URLs, semantic headings and zoom-enabled responsive layouts.
- Added domain regression tests, content validation and browser QA.
